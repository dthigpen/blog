# A More Intentional Phone
tags: dumbphone, phone

Like a lot people, I use a smartphone for just about everything. Texting, navigation, news, podcasts, audiobooks, banking, tickets, two-factor authentication, password managers, notes, and increasingly AI. Its hard to think of another device so central to modern life.

Smartphones aren't bad. They're probably the most useful devices I've owned, but the problem is that the same device I use for the necessities of communication, directions, and information is also the one that offers distraction, entertainment, and instant gratification.

A few weeks ago, I decided to switch to a dumb phone.

## The Challenges

### Navigation

Navigating without a smartphone has proven to be the biggest challenge for me. I think because it forces me to come up with a different routine, or make an exception and carry an additional smartphone with me as a navigation device. Currently, I've only explored the latter. When I go out alone (and cannot rely on my partner's smartphone or car for navigation) I end up just bringing my smartphone along. Doesn't feel great and is annoying to lug around but it works in a pinch.

The next thing I want to try is adopting a routine to totally eliminate the need for a smartphone. This might involve looking at directions on a computer before I leave, printing or writing them down, and building up a memory of the streets and landmarks around me.

### Communication

Upon initially switching away from a smartphone there were some issues with not recieving (RCS) group chat messages. I thought I had done everything right, disabling RCS before switching, but nonetheless I still missed out on messages for a bit before things started fully working again.

Aside from that, texting and group chats have not been a big issue. I might ask for a call a bit more often due to the increased burden of typing but its not too bad.

### Idle Time

Previously I think I would spend just about every minute of idle time either on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) or chatting with some AI about a future project. Hardly ever did I just "sit" with my surroundings.
